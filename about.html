About She Skates

Our Story

She Skates began informally in Vaal, Sebokeng when a small group of female skateboarders started hosting weekend meetups so girls and women could learn to skate without the intimidation often felt in male-dominated skate parks

Our Mission

She Skates exists to give girls and women in Vaal a safe, welcoming space to learn to skate
