## Project Overview
She Skates is a non-profit organisation based in Vaal and Sebokeng, offering girls and women a safe, welcoming space to learn to skate — free from the intimidation often felt in male-dominated skate parks. This website was built as a static, multi-page HTML/CSS/JS site to introduce the organisation online, showcase its programmes, and allow visitors to enquire about volunteering, sponsoring, or getting in touch.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- Introduce visitors to She Skates' story and mission in a clear, welcoming way.
- Present the organisation's skating programmes in enough detail for a prospective participant, volunteer, or sponsor to understand what's on offer.
- Provide a working enquiry form specifically aimed at volunteers and sponsors, since this is an NPO.
- Give contact details for both the Vaal and Sebokeng locations.
- Keep the site simple, readable, and easy to navigate across devices.

## Key Features and Functionality
- **5 HTML pages:** Home, About Us, Our Programmes, Get Involved (Enquiry), Contact.
- **Consistent navigation** across every page, linking back to Home via the "She Skates" logo text.
- **Enquiry form** (`enquiry.html`) letting visitors choose whether they're interested in volunteering or sponsoring.
- **Contact page** (`contact.html`) with two physical locations (Vaal and Sebokeng) and a general contact form.
- **Shared stylesheet** (`css/style.css`) giving the site a consistent pink/dark colour scheme, styled header and navigation, and styled form fields and buttons.
- Comments included in the HTML to explain the main sections of each page.
